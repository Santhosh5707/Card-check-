Two separate screening tools. Each result has its own limitations.

01 — CARD IMAGE

Card image screening

Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 10 MB.

Image classification requires a trained counterfeit-card model and reliable labeled images. Image screening alone cannot prove authenticity.
02 — TRANSACTION DATA

Fraud-risk prediction

Paste transaction rows as JSON. Feature names must match those used to train your model.

A trained transaction model is required. A risk score is an estimate, not proof of fraud.
LIMITATIONS + PRIVACY

What Card Check can verify

  • Image screening can identify visual or quality concerns, but a photo cannot establish whether a card is genuine, active, stolen or authorized.
  • Transaction fraud prediction requires a separately trained and evaluated machine-learning model.
  • Authoritative card verification requires an authorized payment processor or issuer.
  • Do not submit real card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates or personal data.
